Questions to investigate
=========================

CORE CASE QUESTIONS (from the exercise brief -- must answer)
--------------------------------------------------------------
1. Channel behavior: How do offline-, online- and omni-channel customers shop,
   and what sets them apart?

2. Sustainability: How and why do certain categories differ, and what actions
   can Coop take to be more sustainable? (Hint: use the article flags)

3. Customer segmentation: What's the difference between our customer groups,
   and how should Coop act on that?

4. Something else interesting (open).
